Font names: Qutex Math / QutexMath-Regular.
Changes from Latin Modern Math 1.959: identity and provenance metadata; WOFF2
packaging; RadicalVerticalGap 50 -> 250 and RadicalDisplayStyleVerticalGap
148 -> 250 (1000 units/em). Outlines, advances, all other MATH data, kerning
and Unicode coverage are unchanged. Numeric inline radical spacing was compared
with an independent native Hancom output; whole-font equivalence is not claimed.
